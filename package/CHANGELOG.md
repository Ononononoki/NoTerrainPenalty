## 1.0.0

- First release.
- Only creatures count toward the melee multi-hit damage penalty. The ground, walls, rocks, trees and building pieces no longer lower damage to enemies.
- Hitting several enemies keeps the vanilla penalty. Damage to objects is unchanged.
- Config for primary and secondary attacks, excluded skills and excluded items, synced from the server.
